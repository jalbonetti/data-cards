Your slip
How this is built. Every +EV market on the board for the games you ticked, at the best price among your books, with both sides of a market never both taken and one line per player-prop. Then the tracker's rule: ranked by recommended stake (¼-Kelly) or EV %, stake = Kelly % × bankroll, capped per bet and by the daily budget, stop when the budget is spent. Settings are remembered in this browser only. Odds move — check the price at the book before you bet. This is information, not advice; what you can legally bet depends on where you are.
